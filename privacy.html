Privacy policy for Margin Atlas

This page explains how Margin Atlas handles website usage data, advertising technologies, calculator inputs, and contact requests.

Information we may collect

Margin Atlas may collect limited non-personal information such as browser type, general device information, referring pages, approximate location based on browser settings, and standard usage events that help us understand which tools are most useful.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, subject line, and feedback details.

Cookies and advertising

Margin Atlas uses Google AdSense and may display advertising from Google or other approved partners. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, measure performance, and limit duplicate impressions.

Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. Users can learn more about how Google uses data in advertising and can manage ad personalization through their Google ad settings.

Calculator data

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. Margin Atlas does not currently require account creation and does not intentionally store the values you enter into calculator fields on a server.

Third-party services

The website may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, hosting, security, and performance delivery. Those services may process limited technical data as part of operating the site.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may also choose not to use the contact links if you do not want to send email information to us.

Contact

If you have any privacy questions, suggestions, or improvements for Margin Atlas, you can reach us directly by email.

If a privacy detail looks unclear or a tool behavior needs correction, email us and we will review it.
