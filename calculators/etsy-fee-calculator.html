How this Etsy fee calculator works

This calculator adds the product price and shipping charged to the customer, applies a transaction fee percentage, then applies a payment processing percentage and fixed fee. It also includes the listing fee, then subtracts your product cost to estimate remaining profit.

For real-world use, you may also want to account for packaging, shipping labels, taxes, and discounts. Those can be added later as optional inputs if you want a more advanced version.

How to use the result

Start with your item price, shipping amount, and product cost. Then compare the estimated profit with your target margin before publishing the listing.

If the profit is too low, test a higher item price, lower shipping subsidy, or a lower production cost until the numbers are workable.

Who should use this Etsy fee calculator

This tool is useful for handmade sellers, digital product creators, and small online shops that want to estimate listing fees, transaction charges, payment processing costs, and remaining profit before setting a final price.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include all Etsy costs?

It covers the most common fee inputs, but sellers may also want to consider packaging, shipping labels, discounts, ads, and local taxes.

Can I use this for international pricing?

Yes. It works as a planning tool for many markets, but final fees may vary depending on country, payment settings, and Etsy policy updates.
