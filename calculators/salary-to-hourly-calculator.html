Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator include taxes?

No. It converts gross salary figures into hourly, weekly, and monthly estimates. Take-home pay depends on local tax and payroll deductions.

Can I change weekly hours and working weeks?

Yes. Adjusting those inputs is one of the best ways to compare different job structures more accurately.
