About

Margin Atlas is a small set of practical money calculators.

Use it when you need a quick estimate for seller fees, payment fees, salary conversion, freelance pricing, VAT, or GST.

What this site covers

Margin Atlas focuses on calculators that support real business actions: setting product prices, estimating platform fees, pricing freelance projects, and understanding take-home revenue.

Each page is designed to answer one practical question before you make a pricing, invoice, tax, or pay comparison decision.

The site is intentionally limited to a smaller set of finance-adjacent tools so each page can stay practical, easier to review, and easier to improve over time.

What makes the site useful

Each calculator puts the inputs and result near the top of the page, so you can calculate first and read the notes only if you need context.

The pages stay lightweight so they load quickly on mobile and do not require an account.

How calculators are selected

Margin Atlas prioritizes tools that support real work: setting prices, estimating fees, reviewing invoice totals, comparing job offers, and planning sustainable freelance rates.

The site avoids filler pages and does not aim to publish calculators that only generate clicks without helping a practical decision.

How to read the results responsibly

The calculators are best used as planning estimates. Final numbers may differ because of platform rules, country-specific fees, tax treatment, exchange rates, discounts, or account-level settings.

Where exact compliance matters, Margin Atlas encourages users to verify the final number in the official workflow that controls the transaction.
